flightScenario signal · F-02

Orivo Air schedules 14% more seats on the Vela–Iskar corridor

What changed

The fictional schedule shows 14% more scheduled seats on Vela–Iskar for the next six weeks than its preceding file.

Conditional implication

Additional scheduled capacity could affect available fares if the seats operate and demand does not absorb them. No fare or booking outcome is assumed.

Reasoning and falsifier

First mover: Planned seat supply rises on one corridor for a six-week period.

Transmission: Operated capacity could widen flight choice, then influence fares after demand responds.

Assumed OTA exposure: Avenra is assumed to display this corridor; no sales or customer data is used.

Evidence still needed: Operated schedules and observed public fares after the additional flights start.

Falsifier: The filed seats do not operate, or observed capacity and fares show no material change over the stated period.
