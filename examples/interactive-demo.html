System concept / portfolio view

From evidence to a readable brief

A compact view of the product logic illustrated here. The actual repository has a more detailed pipeline and private operational context.

01 / COLLECT

Capture sources

Gather public travel announcements, schedules, market data and selected background material with dates and provenance.

02 / STRUCTURE

Resolve developments

Normalize articles, identify the underlying event and reduce repeated coverage of the same development.

03 / ANALYSE

Trace transmission

Connect demand, supply or price changes to a specific part of an OTA's content and distribution stack.

04 / PUBLISH

Show evidence

Separate the reported change from a conditional implication, display source dates and leave decisions to people.

Design rule: The headline states the source's central fact. The hypothesis states its conditions and missing evidence. Air and hotel both appear in the edition.
