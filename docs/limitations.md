# Scope and limitations

- **Fictional world.** Avenra Travel, Caelora, their markets, product portfolio, source cards and distribution channels are invented. The demo does not represent a real client or disclose any client's private context.
- **No private business data.** No customer records, bookings, conversion, internal sales, partner terms or private correspondence are used.
- **Reference pipeline and curated view.** The offline `reference_pipeline/` demonstrates selected ingest, lineage and render controls; the full visual brief is separately curated. This copy does not operate a scheduled collection pipeline, send email, run live model calls or serve a production application.
- **Illustration versus evidence.** This demo's dated source cards are embedded fictional artifacts, not independent verification of real-world events. A real edition requires original public links and as-of dates for each factual claim.
- **Conditional interpretation.** OTA effects depend on inventory access, customer mix, competitive response and timing. Without private operational data, the system can formulate hypotheses but cannot measure outcomes or prescribe decisions.
- **Editorial judgment.** Automated extraction and ranking can miss an event, merge distinct events or overstate a source. Source checks and human review remain necessary before a real brief is distributed.

The portfolio demonstrates a method and interface. It does not assert that a business benefited from the method.
