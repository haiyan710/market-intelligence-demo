# Sample case: reading two related developments across product lines

This is a **fictional teaching scenario**. The company, market event and source record below are invented to show the analytical format. They must not be quoted as real news or included in a live edition.

## Scenario assumption

An imagined carrier announces extra capacity to a leisure destination on Caelora. At the same time, an imagined hotel group announces new rooms there. Avenra Travel is assumed to distribute flights and hotels in that market.

## How the analysis would read

| Layer | Flight signal | Hotel signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-world record in this sample | A fictional carrier's dated capacity card | A fictional hotel group's dated room-supply card |
| First market variable | Seat supply | Room supply |
| Possible transmission | More seats → wider itinerary choice; fare effects depend on demand and load factors | More rooms → wider stay choice; rate effects depend on occupancy and positioning |
| OTA hypothesis | Available flight content may broaden if the announced service is distributed through the OTA's assumed channels | Relevant hotel choice may broaden if the property is available through the OTA's assumed accommodation channels |
| Evidence that would weaken it | Service is delayed, outside the OTA's channels, or rapidly absorbed by demand | Opening is delayed, inventory is unavailable to the OTA, or new supply is immaterial to its target segment |

The offline sample can cite its embedded fictional cards. A real-world brief would instead require dated, clickable public sources and would state only the facts those sources support. The conditional links above are analytical possibilities, not forecasts or recommendations.
